# Quality Management System (QMS) - ISO 9001:2015
**Project:** Interceptor_M
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Scope
This QMS covers the design, development, and prototyping of the Interceptor_M drone swarm and compressed air launcher system.

## 2. Design Control
- **Inputs:** All requirements are documented in `Cahier_Charges_Prototype.md` and `PARAMETERS.json`.
- **Outputs:** Verified STL models and electronic schematics generated by versioned scripts.
- **Reviews:** Formal Gate Reviews (G0-G11) moderated by the Agent Manager.
- **Verification:** Simulation-driven performance validation (6-DOF, CFD).

## 3. Document Control
- All technical documents must start with IAMD YAML metadata.
- Version history must be maintained in `STATUS-REPORT.md` and `DECISION_LOG.md`.

## 4. Continuous Improvement
- Internal audits performed by the AC Agent.
- Feedback loops integrated into every Gate phase.

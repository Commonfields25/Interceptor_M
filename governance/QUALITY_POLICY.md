# 📜 PROJECT QUALITY POLICY (ISO 9001:2015)

## 1. Commitment
The Interceptor_M program is committed to delivering autonomous drone systems that meet the highest standards of safety, reliability, and precision. We achieve this through a process-driven approach integrated directly into our AI Agent orchestration.

## 2. Core Principles
- **Continuous Improvement**: Every technical failure or process bottleneck is analyzed by the AC Agent to prevent recurrence.
- **Traceability**: No modification to the code or design exists without an audit trail (Agent ID, Timestamp, Gate Approval).
- **Risk-Based Thinking**: All designs undergo FEA and constraint verification before moving to physical prototyping.

## 3. Objectives
- 100% adherence to **ISO 9001** and **AS9100** standards by Q4 2027.
- Zero critical safety incidents during flight testing.
- MTOW deviations < 5% from target baseline.

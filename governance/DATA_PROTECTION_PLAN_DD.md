# Data Protection Plan - Defense Line (DD)
**Project:** Interceptor_M
**Segment:** Defense (P3)

## 1. Objective
Protect the intellectual property and strategic advantage of the "Micro-Interceptor Swarm" concept.

## 2. Protected Assets
- **CAD Geometry:** Specific dimensions of the 35mm fuselage and SABOT-001 interface.
- **Physics Models:** 6-DOF simulation parameters and flight control algorithms.
- **Mission Logic:** Swarm coordination and intercept scenarios.

## 3. Storage & Encryption
- All DD-line artifacts are stored in restricted namespaces.
- Any export for external review must be password-protected or encrypted.

## 4. Transmission
- No sharing of raw DD STLs or STEP files over unencrypted channels.
- DG validation required for any external technical presentation.
